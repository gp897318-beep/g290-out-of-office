A single line (79 characters) out-of-office email auto-reply, written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8) "Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply".

## The entry

> Out of office: your email will be answered in the order of how much I like you.

Ten candidates were drafted and compared against the judge's five criteria (a real one-line OOO reply first, then a laugh on the first read, then reading like a genuine away message, then a fresh twist, then short with every word earning its place): a Roomba watching the inbox, reply order by how much I like you, the auto-reply also out of office, fishing for a Wi-Fi signal, the inbox unionised, taking the urgent button, a meeting with my sofa, finding myself / return to sender, the calendar ate me, emails needing privacy. The reply-order line won: it is the only candidate scoring top marks on criteria 2, 3, 4 and 5 at once — the laugh lands instantly (every reader ranks themselves in the queue), the queue promise is the away message rather than a joke bolted onto one, the twist is fresh (affection as a sorting key, nothing to do with beaches, inboxes or "limited email"), and at 79 characters every word earns its place. Runner-up was taking the urgent button — equally short and real-message-shaped, but clever rather than funny.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt` (absolute path, as the competition requires) after checking the gate inside the program: UTF-8 text, 1–160 characters, a single line, not only whitespace.
